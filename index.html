ABOUT US

Working together for a better society.

“Sath Chale Ham” started as a small effort with the aim of helping people in need and promoting equality in society.

Our goal is to reach children, women and families with education, assistance, awareness and opportunities so that people can move towards a dignified and self-reliant life.
